Colour psychology: why these colours

  • Navy: authority, stability and calm. Used for headings, the process band and outdoor zones. Security buyers read it as serious and dependable.
  • Royal blue: trust and action. Used only for things you can click, and for camera markers, so blue always means "interact" or "camera".
  • Cyan: clarity and technology. Used for coverage and common areas, and for progress lines.
  • Green: safe, recorded, supported. Used only for the recording room, the recording dot and the support band.
  • Orange: attention and entry points. Used sparingly: entrances, the Nadiad dot, and the end tick of each section line. Never for buttons or body text.
  • Pale blue-white backgrounds: keep the page clean and technical, and keep contrast above WCAG AA for all text.
